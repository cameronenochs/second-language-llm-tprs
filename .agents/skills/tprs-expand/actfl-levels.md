# ACTFL level calibration for TPRS dialogues

Starting points based on ACTFL proficiency descriptors. The user may override any value.

| Level | Max words per sentence | Grammar scope | New vocabulary per paragraph of source | Question types | Teacher restatement vs. new content |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Novice Low | 5 | Present tense only; one clause; no subordination | 2-3 | Either/or and yes/no only | Restate every new fact 3 times before moving on |
| Novice Mid | 7 | Present tense; simple negation; basic adjectives | 3-4 | Either/or, yes/no, simple who/what | Restate each new fact twice |
| Novice High | 10 | Present plus one past or future form; coordinated clauses with "and", "but" | 4-5 | Adds where/when/why-simple | Restate each new fact twice, shorter the second time |
| Intermediate Low | 12 | Main past and future tenses; simple subordinate clauses (because, when) | 5-6 | Adds open wh- questions about earlier content | Restate once, then move on |
| Intermediate Mid | 16 | Full range of common tenses; relative clauses; some reported speech | 6-8 | Open questions, "why" with reasons, opinion questions | Restate only at genuinely hard spots |
| Intermediate High | 20 | Complex sentences; connected paragraph-length turns; some subjunctive or equivalent | 8-10 | Open questions, hypotheticals, retelling requests | Rarely restate; Learner retells in own words |

Notes:
- "New vocabulary per paragraph" counts items the Learner has not met earlier in the dialogue.
- Either/or questions about just-narrated content stay at every level; only the sentence length and grammar inside them scale.
